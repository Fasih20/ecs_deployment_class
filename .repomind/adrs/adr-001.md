# ADR-001: Establish Initial Architectural Baseline

**Status:** proposed

## Context

The recent codebase scan indicates the initial establishment of an architectural baseline. This signifies the first formal documentation of the project's structure and intended design principles. It's crucial to capture this as the foundational understanding of the system's architecture.

## Decision

The project will adopt a modular architecture, with clear separation of concerns between different components. This initial baseline serves as the reference point for all future architectural discussions and changes.

## Consequences

- Provides a clear understanding of the system's structure for new developers.
- Facilitates consistent development practices and reduces architectural drift.
- Enables easier identification of deviations from the intended design.
- May require initial effort to define and document the baseline thoroughly.

---

Related tasks: Define and document the initial module boundaries., Identify key architectural principles and patterns to be followed., Establish a process for reviewing and updating the architectural baseline.
